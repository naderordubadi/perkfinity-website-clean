Merchant FAQ

Questions Merchants Ask

Everything you want to know before signing up.

Do my employees need any training?

+

No. Your staff does absolutely nothing different. Customers scan the QR stand on their own phone while waiting in line. The only thing a cashier does at checkout is see the offer on the customer's phone, honor the discount, and tap — or ask the customer to tap — "Mark as Redeemed" on their phone. That's it. No new device, no app on the register.

Do I need to buy or install any hardware?

+

No hardware purchase required. You place a branded Perkfinity QR display at your counter — that's your entire physical setup. No tablets, no scanners, no subscriptions to hardware. Everything else runs on the customer's own phone.

Does it work with my existing POS system?

+

Yes — universally. Because everything happens inside the customer's app, Perkfinity runs alongside any POS you already use: Toast, Clover, Square, Lightspeed, or anything else. No integration, no IT work, no touchpoints with your existing system.

What's the difference between campaigns and announcements?

+

Campaigns are offer-based (e.g. "10% off your next visit" or "Free dessert with entree") — customers scan, receive the offer, and show it at checkout. Announcements are informational messages you push to your members (e.g. new hours, a sale starting Friday, or a holiday closure). Both are sent to members via email and push notification automatically.

How hard is it to create a campaign or announcement?

+

It takes just a few minutes and requires zero marketing experience. Our dashboard walks you through a simple step-by-step guided flow: choose the type (campaign or announcement), fill in the details (title, offer or message, and duration), preview exactly how it will appear to your members, and publish. That's it. No design skills, no writing from scratch — the wizard guides every step and you can go from idea to live in under 5 minutes.

How does Perkfinity actually send my campaigns and announcements to members?

+

Perkfinity uses a smart Daily Digest system. Every morning, each member receives a single email and a single push notification — one of each, no more — that bundles together every new perk and announcement from all the stores they belong to. This means even if a member has joined ten local businesses, they'll never get bombarded with ten separate emails. They get one clean, personalized digest curated specifically for them. Your new campaign or announcement is always included — it's simply delivered as part of a respectful, member-friendly daily update that people actually look forward to opening.

What happens when I reach 100 members?

+

Your account automatically upgrades to Tier 1 ($29.99/month) when your 100th member joins. You don't need to do anything. Billing starts from that point forward. You can also manually upgrade at any time from your dashboard if you'd prefer.

Do I ever see my customers' personal information?

+

No identifying personal information — ever. You will never see a customer's name, email address, or phone number. What you do see in your dashboard is an anonymized member ID for each check-in, along with their city and zip code for analytics purposes — so you can understand where your members are coming from and make smarter decisions about your campaigns. That's it. Perkfinity holds all personal data — you have zero privacy liability and zero compliance burden.

Can I send announcements if I don't want to run coupons?

+

Absolutely. Announcements are available independently of campaigns. If you're a business that simply wants to communicate with your customer base — say a dental office notifying patients of availability, or a gym updating class schedules — you can use Perkfinity purely for announcements without ever running a discount offer.

Can I cancel anytime?

+

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time directly from your merchant dashboard — no need to call or email us. When you cancel, you keep full access until the end of your current 30-day billing period.

Member FAQ

Questions Members Ask

Everything you want to know before downloading.

Is Perkfinity free for members?

+

Yes, completely free — forever. No subscriptions, no fees, no freemium traps. You download, sign up, and start accessing perks at no cost.

Do stores see my personal information?

+

No personal information — ever. Merchants never see your name, email address, or phone number. For analytics purposes, stores can see general location data (city and zip code) from their member base — but this is fully anonymized and not traceable to any specific member. It's aggregate data only, used to help merchants understand where their customers are generally coming from. Perkfinity holds your personal information and never shares it with merchants.

How do I redeem a perk at checkout?

+

At checkout, when it's your turn, open Perkfinity and select your desired offer — you may have more than one available for the same store, so pick the one you want to use. Activate the offer and show it on your phone to the cashier. They'll honor it and tap — or ask you to tap — "Mark as Redeemed" on your phone. No codes, no printing, no separate device.

Note: Once you activate an offer, a 5-minute countdown clock starts. If it reaches zero, the offer expires for good. If you're not ready yet — say it's not your turn — tap Done / Cancel before the timer runs out to exit without redeeming, then activate it again when you're ready.

Does it work at multiple stores or just one?

+

One app works at all participating local businesses. You join each store individually when you scan their QR code for the first time, and from then on you'll see their offers and receive their notifications automatically.

What kind of perks will I get?

+

Each store creates its own perks — they're specific to that location and curated for their members. Think percentage discounts, free items with purchase, exclusive new customer offers, or early access to promotions. They're real, local deals — not generic points that take years to matter.

Do I need a plastic card or loyalty number?

+

No. Your phone is your loyalty card, your coupon book, and your membership — all in one app. No physical card to carry, no number to recite at the register, no app to download per store.

Will I get spammed with emails and notifications?

+

Absolutely not — and this is something we designed very carefully. Instead of sending you a separate email every time one of your stores posts a new offer, Perkfinity delivers a single Daily Digest each morning: one email and one push notification that rounds up every new perk and announcement from all the stores you've joined. Think of it like a personalized morning briefing — a quick glance and you instantly know what deals are waiting for you today across all your favorite local spots. No inbox flooding, no notification fatigue, no noise. Just one clean, curated update — only when there's something new worth knowing about.

Can I delete my account? What happens to my data?

+

Yes — you can delete your account at any time directly from the Perkfinity app with no friction. When you do, we permanently delete all of your personally identifiable information (PII): your name, email address, phone number, and any other details that could identify you. What we retain is fully anonymized activity data — such as aggregate check-in counts and redemption statistics — stripped of any connection to you personally. This retained data cannot be traced back to you in any way and is kept solely for analytics and legal compliance purposes. Your privacy is ours to protect, even after you leave.
