Our Mission

Perks without the friction — or the compromise.

Perkfinity was built on a simple belief: getting a great deal from a brand you love — or discovering a hidden gem around the corner — shouldn't require giving up your privacy, downloading a store-specific app, or jumping between different apps and sites to find online and local deals.

Our mission is to connect people with the businesses around them — from the neighborhood coffee shop to the online brand they follow — through perks that are simple to use and private by design. Whether the deal comes from a local store down the street or a mainstream brand online, it lands in one place. And throughout, one rule never changes: we never share your personal information with the merchants you interact with.

Our Vision

Local roots. Global reach.

Community commerce starts at the neighborhood level — the food truck on your street, the independent bookstore downtown, the local spa you've been meaning to try. Perkfinity is designed to make those connections frictionless: scan, join, earn perks, repeat.

But community doesn't stop at your zip code. As Perkfinity grows, so does the network — regional chains, national ecommerce brands, and large enterprise retailers all belong on the same platform, serving the same communities. Whether you shop in person, online, or both, Perkfinity is the bridge between you and the businesses that want to reward your loyalty.

We envision a world where a neighborhood bakery and a national apparel brand both have equal access to a simple, powerful tool for building real customer relationships — and where a shopper can discover both in one place, without sacrificing privacy for either.

Our Story

Started local. Built for everyone.

Perkfinity started with a simple frustration: local businesses had no affordable way to offer digital perks, and consumers had no single place to find them. Loyalty platforms and systems were expensive and complex, and commission-based platforms ate into already thin margins.

We built Perkfinity to fix that — starting with QR-based perks for local and mobile merchants, then expanding to online brands and hybrid businesses that want both digital reach and in-store redemption. Today, Perkfinity supports four merchant types across the US, with an Enterprise tier for multi-location brands that need store-specific performance and centralized control. A growing community of members discovers new perks every day.

Why Perkfinity

Not just another coupons app.

🔖
vs. Groupon
Zero commissions. You own your customer relationships.

Groupon takes up to 50% of every deal sold and owns the customer relationship. Perkfinity charges a flat monthly plan — merchants keep 100% of every sale and maintain a direct, privacy-safe connection with their own member base.

💳
vs. Loyalty Programs
No new hardware. No integration. No employee training.

Traditional loyalty systems require new hardware, POS integrations, and staff training before a single customer can use them. Perkfinity runs on a printable QR stand and the customer's own phone. Setup takes minutes — not months — and your team doesn't need to learn anything new.

🔒
vs. Browser Extensions & Coupon Sites
Privacy first — for members and merchants.

Browser coupon tools track everything you browse and buy. Perkfinity is the opposite: members reveal a promo code in their phone and use it — no browsing data collected, no profile built, and no personal information shared with the business offering the deal.

🌐
vs. Online-Only Coupon Platforms
Local + online + enterprise, in one place.

Online coupon platforms are great for ecommerce but ignore the local bakery and the food truck. Perkfinity covers all four business types — online, hybrid, physical, and mobile — plus an Enterprise tier for multi-location brands. One app, one community.
