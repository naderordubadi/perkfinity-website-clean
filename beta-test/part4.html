Returning Member Tester Guide

Test the login flow for a user who already has the app installed — run this as a separate session after completing Part 2.

👋 Welcome back, Tester!

This guide covers Part 4: Returning Member — testing the experience of a user who already has the Perkfinity app and logs back in after scanning a new QR code.

  • ⚠️ Please complete Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3 before starting this guide.
  • You need an existing consumer account from Part 2 and an active campaign created in Part 3.
  • This test takes approximately 3–5 minutes.
  • Report any issues to hello@perkfinity.net.
⚠️ Prerequisites: You must have completed Parts 1, 2, and 3. You need an existing Perkfinity consumer account (Part 2), an active merchant campaign (Part 3), and the app installed via TestFlight. Sign out before starting this test.
🔄

Part 4: Returning Member

Verify the app correctly preserves QR context through the log-in flow

A1
Scan the Merchant QR Code & Log In

This tests whether the app correctly remembers a pending offer even when the user logs in after scanning:

  1. Sign out of the app — the Sign Out button is on the Home screen (top-right corner)
  2. Scan the same merchant QR code from Part 1 with your iPhone camera
  3. On the onboarding slides, tap Skip or progress through to the auth screen
  4. Tap "Log In" and enter your email and password
  5. Tap Log In
💡 Expected Result: After logging in, the Home screen should immediately show the pending offer banner — confirming the app preserved the QR scan context through the log-in flow.
A2
Loading & Offer Detection

After tapping Log In, watch for these states:

  1. The Log In button changes to "Please wait..." (loading feedback)
  2. A full-screen loading state appears: "Loading your offers..." with an hourglass animation
  3. The app lands on the Home screen with your offers loaded
⚠️ Check: The pending offer from the QR scan should appear in the Available Perks section — confirming the offer context was preserved through log-in.
A3
Offer Expiration & Status Sync

Verify that ignoring the timer securely expires the offer on both ends:

  1. On the Home screen, note how many offers are listed in the Available Perks section before proceeding
  2. Tap your pending offer to open the details view
  3. Tap Activate Offer
  4. The 5-minute countdown timer will begin. (Let this timer run out entirely without tapping Done)
  5. Once expired, check the app first before the dashboard:
    • Tap the History tab — confirm the expired offer appears there with an Expired status
    • Return to the Home screen — confirm the number of available offers went down by one
  6. Then open the Merchant Dashboard on your computer
  7. Navigate to Member List and confirm the member's status changed to Expired
💡 Expected Result: The app History shows the offer as Expired, the Home screen available count decreases by 1, and the Member List in the Merchant Dashboard shows the status as Expired.
A4
Cancellation & Status Reversal

Test the "Done/Cancel" fallback to make sure accidental activations can be undone:

  1. Get to a pending offer:
    • If you are still logged in — go to the Home screen; your remaining pending offers will already be listed there
    • If you are not logged in — scan the merchant QR code again and log in to access your pending offers
  2. Tap the offer and tap Activate Offer to start the timer
  3. After a couple of seconds, tap the Done or Cancel button in the UI
⚠️ Check: The offer should immediately revert to the Available Perks pending queue, ensuring members don't lose accidental activations.
A5
Background Interruption

Test how the app handles navigation away from an active timer:

  1. Get to a pending offer:
    • If you are still logged in — go to the Home screen; your remaining pending offers will already be listed there
    • If you are not logged in — scan the merchant QR code again and log in to access your pending offers
  2. Tap the offer and tap Activate Offer to start the timer
  3. While the timer is actively counting down, tap the Profile tab or navigate away
  4. Return to the Home screen
💡 Expected Result: The app should safely catch the interruption and revert the offer to a "Pending" state in your list.
