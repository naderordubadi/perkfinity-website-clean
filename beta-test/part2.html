Member Experience Tester Guide

Step-by-step walkthrough for testing the Perkfinity consumer (member) app — from first scan to your first redeemed perk.

👋 Welcome, Tester!

This guide walks you through the Member (Consumer) experience — the app that everyday shoppers use to discover perks and redeem discounts at local merchants.

  • You will scan a merchant QR code, download the Perkfinity app, sign up, and redeem your first perk
  • The test takes approximately 5–10 minutes end-to-end
  • Use an iPhone with iOS 16+ and a working Apple ID
  • Report any issues at hello@perkfinity.net
⚠️ Prerequisites: Before starting this guide, ensure you have a merchant QR code available — either printed from the Merchant Sign-Up flow or displayed on a screen. If you don't have one, complete the Merchant Tester Guide (Part 1) first, or ask your test coordinator for a QR code.

Phase A: Discover & Install

Scanning the QR code, auto-redirecting to TestFlight, downloading, and opening the app

🎯
What to Watch For
  • QR → TestFlight Redirect: Does scanning the QR code with the native camera app automatically open TestFlight (via a brief Safari hop)? This is the key thing to confirm.
  • Invitation in TestFlight: If the tester has a pending invitation, does it appear inside TestFlight ready to accept?
  • Invitation Email Fallback: If no invitation appears in TestFlight, can the tester find and use the invitation link from their email?
  • App Installation: Does the app install without errors after accepting the invitation?
  • Onboarding Slides on Launch: After opening the app for the first time, do the 3 onboarding slides appear immediately (not the home screen)?
A1
Scan the Merchant QR Code

Open your iPhone's built-in Camera app and point it at the merchant's QR code (printed sign or screen). No third-party scanner needed.

  • A yellow notification banner appears at the bottom of the screen — tap it
  • Safari opens briefly and immediately redirects — you do not need to interact with it
  • The phone is automatically handed off to TestFlight
💡 Tip: Keep the QR code well-lit and centered in frame. Detection usually takes 1–2 seconds. The Safari → TestFlight redirect is automatic and fast.
⚠️ What we're verifying: That scanning with the regular iOS Camera app correctly triggers the TestFlight redirect. If it does not open TestFlight, note this as a bug.
A2
Accept Invitation & Install in TestFlight

Once TestFlight opens, one of two scenarios will apply:

  1. Invitation is already in TestFlight — If you were previously invited, the Perkfinity beta will appear in your TestFlight app list. Tap Install.
  2. No invitation visible — Go to your email inbox and find the TestFlight invitation email from Perkfinity (sent to you by the test coordinator). Tap the "View in TestFlight" link in that email, then tap Accept → Install.
💡 Don't have TestFlight? If TestFlight isn't installed on your phone, the App Store will open first — download TestFlight (free), then return to your invitation email link to install Perkfinity.
💡 TestFlight Note: You may briefly see "This beta isn't accepting any new testers." — this is a cosmetic TestFlight quirk. The installation proceeds normally; tap past it.
A3
Open the App — Confirm Home Screen

After installation completes, tap Open in TestFlight (or open Perkfinity from your home screen).

Expected result: The app opens to the general Home screen. You should see participating merchants and the Available Perks section — but no merchant-specific pending offer banner, since you are not signed up / logged in yet.

💡 Note: If a pending offer does appear at this point, note it — that would mean the QR session was unexpectedly preserved into the install. For the purposes of this test, proceed normally either way.
🔑

Phase B: Account Creation & Onboarding

Camera permissions, sign-up, profile, and final permissions

🎯
What to Watch For
  • Camera Permission: Does the permission dialog appear with correct wording? Does Allow/Cancel work as expected?
  • Sign-Up Options: Are all three sign-up methods visible (Apple, Google, Email)?
  • Form Validation: Do password requirements check in real time (✓ green checks appear as criteria are met)?
  • Error States: If you submit an incomplete form, does a clear error appear?
  • Profile Fields: Do Name, Phone, City, and ZIP fields accept input correctly?
  • Location Setting: In the Permissions screen, is the Enable Location checkbox pre-checked? Can it be unchecked and re-checked? (Keep it enabled for testing.)
  • Push Notifications Setting: Is the Allow Notifications checkbox pre-checked? Can it be unchecked and re-checked? (Keep it enabled for testing.)
  • Post-Permissions destination: After tapping Continue on the Permissions screen, does the app go directly to the Scan screen?
B1
Camera Access Permission

The first screen after launch is a camera permission request:

  • Title: "Camera Access Needed"
  • Description: "Perkfinity needs your camera to scan QR codes and unlock discounts at local merchant stores."
  • Tap Allow Camera Access — this is required to scan QR codes in-store later
💡 iOS will show its own native camera permission dialog after you tap Allow. Tap "Allow" there too.
B2
Scan the QR Code to Reach Sign-Up

After granting camera access, the live QR scanner opens:

  • Corner bracket frame with a purple animated scanning line
  • Text: "Align QR Code — Position the merchant QR code within the frame to reveal your reward."

Point your camera at the merchant QR code again. This scan takes you directly to the sign-up page.

💡 Tip: Use the same QR code from Phase A (the printed sign or screen). Hold steady — the scanner will detect it automatically.
B3
Choose Sign-Up Method

A sign-in options screen prompts you to create an account or log in. Three options are available:

  • 🍎 Sign in with Apple
  • 🔵 Sign in with Google
  • 📧 Sign Up with Email (recommended for this test)

At the bottom: "Already registered? Log in" — use this if you already have a Perkfinity account.

💡 For this test, tap Sign Up with Email to test the full registration flow. You can test Apple/Google sign-in in a separate pass.
B4
Create Your Account — Email & Password

Fill in the registration form:

  • Email Address * — Use a real, working email address (e.g. your+test@gmail.com)
  • Password * — Must meet all four requirements shown with live checkmarks:

📝 Write down your credentials. You'll need your email + password to log back in later.

Tap Create Account → when all checkmarks are green.

B5
Complete Your Profile

After account creation, you'll be taken to a profile setup screen. Fill in:

  • Full Name *
  • Phone Number * — auto-formatted as you type (e.g. 714-555-1234)
  • City *
  • ZIP Code *
💡 Tip: Use a real city and ZIP code. This is used to match you with nearby merchants in the Home screen.

Tap Continue → when all fields are filled.

B6
Location & Notification Permissions

Both permissions appear on the same screen with two pre-checked checkboxes:

  • ☑ "Enable Location" — allows Perkfinity to find merchants near you
  • ☑ "Allow Notifications" — lets Perkfinity notify you of new perks and redemption confirmations

Tap Continue → (below the Push Notifications checkbox) to proceed.

💡 Note: You can uncheck either permission — but for the purposes of this test, please leave both checkboxes checked so we can verify the full experience.
B7
App Tour Slides

Three introductory onboarding slides walk you through Perkfinity's value propositions. Tap Continue → on each slide to advance:

  1. 🔒 "Privacy-First" — Your data is encrypted and never shared with merchants
  2. ⚡ "Redeem Instantly" — Show QR code or code to cashier to redeem in seconds
  3. 🏪 "One App, Many Stores" — Perks at a growing network of local merchants

Tap Get Started → on the last slide to land on the Home screen (you're already signed in).

💡 Tip: You can also tap Skip in the top-right corner to go directly to the Home screen.
🏠

Phase C: Home Screen & Discovery

Exploring the home tab, merchants, and your pending perk

🎯
What to Watch For
  • Sign Out button: Does the Sign Out button appear in the top-right corner? Tapping it should log you out and return to the sign-in screen.
  • Hero banner: Is the "Ready to Save at Local Shops?" card visible? Does tapping Get Started → launch the 3 onboarding slides?
  • Gold "Available Offers" banner: Is it visible in the Available Perks section with the correct offer count (e.g. "1 Available")? Does tapping it open the Offers page?
  • Offers page — single offer: Does it show merchant name, offer title, terms, expiration date, and "Activate This Offer" button?
  • Offers page — multiple offers: If more than one offer exists, are ← Prev / Next → buttons and dot indicators visible and working?
  • Expiration color: Is the expiration date color-coded correctly (white = plenty of time, yellow = ending soon, red = within 3 days)?
  • Participating Merchants: Is the most recently scanned merchant showing on the far left? Do other merchant cards appear to the right?
  • Bottom Navigation: Are all 4 tabs (🏠 Home, 📷 Scan, 🕐 History, 👤 Profile) visible and tappable?
C1
Explore the Home Screen

The Home screen has several distinct areas — from top to bottom:

  • Top bar: Perkfinity logo (left) + Sign Out button (right). Tapping Sign Out logs you out and returns you to the sign-in screen.
  • Hero banner (large dark card, center): Displays perk badges (15% off, 20% off, 10% off) and the headline "Ready to Save at Local Shops?" with a green Get Started → button. Tapping Get Started takes you back through the onboarding slides.
  • Available Perks (below hero): Shows a count label on the right (e.g. "1 Available") reflecting how many offers this user has. Beneath it is a gold banner card — e.g. "Buy One Get One Free — Tap to activate your pending offer!" with an arrow →. Tap this card to view and activate the offer.
  • Participating Merchants Around You: A horizontal row of merchant cards. The most recently scanned merchant appears on the far left. Scroll right to see others.

Bottom Navigation Bar

📷Scan
🕐History
👤Profile

The bottom bar matches what's shown in the app: a house icon (Home), QR grid icon (Scan), clock/history icon (History), and person icon (Profile). Tap each tab and verify it navigates correctly.

C2
View Your Available Offers

The only way to reach the Offers page is by tapping the gold "Available Offers" banner in the Available Perks section on the Home screen. There is no tap on individual perk cards.

Once on the Offers page you will see:

  • Merchant name, logo, and location
  • The offer title (e.g. "Buy One Get One Free") and any terms
  • Expiration date for each offer — color-coded: white (plenty of time), yellow (ending soon), red (expiring within 3 days)
  • If you have more than one offer, use the ← Prev and Next → buttons (and the dot indicators) to browse between them
  • A prominent "Activate This Offer" button for each offer
  • A note: "Valid for 5 minutes once activated"
⚠️ Do not tap "Activate This Offer" until you are in front of a cashier (or your test partner acting as cashier). The 5-minute countdown starts immediately and cannot be paused.

Phase D: Activate & Redeem the Perk

The moment of truth — the 5-minute redemption window

🎯
What to Watch For
  • Countdown Timer: Does the 5:00 countdown begin immediately upon activation? Does it count down accurately in real time?
  • QR Code Display: Is a scannable QR code shown on the redemption screen?
  • 6-Digit Code: Is a fallback static code displayed (format: XXX-XXX)?
  • Cashier Button: Is the "Cashier: Mark as Redeemed" button visible and functional?
  • Success Screen: After marking as redeemed, does a "Successfully Redeemed!" message appear?
  • Timer Expiry: If the 5-minute window expires, does the offer gracefully expire with a clear message?
D1
Activate and View the Redemption Screen

Tap Activate This Offer. The Redemption Screen immediately appears showing:

  • A QR code with the merchant's logo in the center (the logo uploaded to their dashboard)
  • A 6-digit code below the QR (e.g. 690-649) — unique to each activation
  • The campaign details — offer name and merchant name
  • A 5-minute countdown timer showing time remaining
⚠️ The 5-minute countdown starts immediately and cannot be paused. The offer expires when the timer hits 0:00.
D2
Mark as Redeemed & Confirm

At the bottom of the Redemption Screen, tap the green button:

Cashier: Mark as Redeemed

After tapping, a brief loading indicator appears, then the success confirmation is shown:

✅ Successfully Redeemed!

The app stays on this screen — there is no automatic redirect. You can tap any tab in the bottom navigation to continue, or close the app.

⚠️ This button must be tapped before the timer hits 0:00. Once expired, the offer can no longer be redeemed.
📋

Phase E: Verify History & Profile

Confirming the redemption is logged and your account is correct

🎯
What to Watch For
  • History Entry: Does the History tab show your redeemed perk with the correct merchant name, offer, and timestamp?
  • Profile Tab: Is your name, phone, city, and ZIP correct? Can you update a field directly and tap Continue → Permissions → Finish Setup to save?
E1
Check the History Tab

Tap the History tab in the bottom navigation bar. You should see:

  • Your redeemed perk listed as an entry (e.g. "Buy One Get One Free — Safebox Savings")
  • The precise redemption timestamp (date and time)
  • Status marked as Redeemed
💡 Cross-check: If you have access to the Merchant Dashboard, confirm the same redemption appears there under the member's record.
E2
Review Your Profile Tab

Tap the Profile tab and verify:

  • Your name, phone, city, and ZIP are correct (no email field on this screen)
  • Fields are directly editable — update any value, tap Continue → walks through Permissions → tap Finish Setup to save
💡 Note: Sign Out is on the Home screen (top-right corner), not on the Profile tab.

📌 Known Beta Notes

TestFlight "Not Accepting Testers" Message: You may briefly see this error in TestFlight. It is a known UI glitch — the app still installs. Tap past it.
5-Minute Redemption Window: This is intentional to prevent offline misuse. Once the timer expires the QR code and fallback code both become invalid. Do not activate an offer unless ready to test the full flow immediately.
💡 Multiple Merchants: You will see other merchants on the Home screen (e.g. "Mission Viejo Bakery", "Ryan O's HQ"). This is an intentional feature to encourage users to discover and visit other participating locations nearby. The merchant you just scanned will always appear first in the list.
