Merchant Sign-Up Tester Guide

Register a test merchant and generate your QR code sign — perkfinity.net

👋 Welcome, Tester!

This guide covers Part 1: Merchant Sign-Up. Please complete all 5 steps on the website and verify your QR code sign is generated.

  • Use a real, working email — needed for login later
  • Use a real ZIP code (e.g. 92692) — needed for search test
  • Report issues to hello@perkfinity.net
⚠️ Save your credentials! Write down your email and password before continuing — you'll need them for Parts 2 and 3.
Phase A — Merchant Registration
→
Getting Started

Open perkfinity.net → click Merchant Sign Up in the top nav. You'll complete a 5-step wizard.

1
Step 1: Store / Business Details
  • Store Name, Address, City, State, ZIP — use real data
  • Email — real working email (your login username)
  • Phone — format xxx-xxx-xxxx
  • New Member Perk — keep default or choose another from dropdown
💡 Click Continue to Tier Selection → to proceed.
2
Step 2: Choose Your Plan

Select Trial Mode (Free). No payment until 10 active members. Click Continue →.

3
Step 3: Create Your Account
  • Password — min 8 chars, 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 number (e.g. TestPass1)
  • Confirm Password — must match
📝 Write down your email & password now — you'll need them in Parts 2 and 3.

Click Create Account & Continue →

4
Step 4: Connect Bank Account (Simulated)
💡 This is a sandbox Plaid simulation — no real bank is connected. Click 🔗 Connect Bank with Plaid and wait for it to complete automatically.
5
Step 5: Upload Store Logo

Click Choose Image File and upload any image (PNG/JPG). Click Finish & Generate Sign →.

⚠️ Upload any image — we need to verify logo appears on the dashboard, QR sign, and consumer app.
✓
Sign-Up Complete — QR Sign Ready!

Your countertop sign is generated with your store name, perk, and QR code.

⚠️ Save your QR! Click 🖨️ Print Countertop Sign → Save as PDF. You need this QR code to scan in Part 2.
📱 Next: Scan the QR code with your phone camera to start Part 2.
Phase B — Merchant Dashboard
B
Merchant Dashboard Access

Go to perkfinity.net → click Merchant Login → enter your email & password from Step 3.

  • View Member List (members who scanned your QR)
  • Update Profile (logo, address, password)
  • Reprint QR Sign from the Profile tab
