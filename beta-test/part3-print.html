Campaign Creation Guide

Requires completing Part 1 (Merchant Sign-Up) and Part 2 (Member Sign-Up) first

👋 Welcome back!

  • ⚠️ Complete Part 1 and Part 2 before starting this guide.
  • Use your merchant account from Part 1 and consumer account from Part 2
  • Estimated time: 5–8 minutes
  • Screenshot the Campaign Creation process and share with us!
  • Report bugs to hello@perkfinity.net
Phase A — Dashboard Login & Profile
A1
Sign In to the Merchant Dashboard
  1. Go to perkfinity.net → click Merchant Sign Up / Sign In in the top nav
  2. Click Sign in here
  3. Enter your merchant email and password (from Part 1) and click Sign In
A2
Update Profile & Replace Logo
  1. Navigate to the Profile tab
  2. Update a few fields (e.g. Website, Suite, Phone)
  3. Click Replace Logo → select a new image file (PNG/JPG)
  4. Scroll down, enter your Current Password, click Save Changes
⚠️ Verify: New logo should immediately update in the top header and persist after page refresh.
Phase B — Campaign Creation
B1
Create Campaign 1 — Percentage Off (All Members)

Navigate to Create a Promotion tab and complete the 6-step wizard:

  1. Type: Percentage Off
  2. Details: Enter discount % (e.g. 20%). Expiration: 3–4 days out
  3. Condition: No Minimum
  4. Delivery: Email + Push
  5. Audience: All Members
  6. Preview & Send: Verify logo & offer → click 🚀 Send Promotion
B2
Create Campaign 2 — Dollar Off (Last 30 Days)

Click Start Over, then:

  1. Type: Dollar Off
  2. Details: $5 Off, expiration a couple days out
  3. Condition: Minimum Spend → enter amount (e.g. 20)
  4. Delivery: Email   Audience: Redeemed — Last 30 Days
  5. Review → 🚀 Send Promotion
B3
Create Campaign 3 — BOGO

Click Start Over, then:

  1. Type: Buy One Get One
  2. Details: Fill in Buy/Get details & item description. Set a future expiry
  3. Condition: Minimum Items — enter same qty as the Buy field (e.g. Buy = 1, enter 1)   Delivery: Push
  4. Audience: All Members → 🚀 Send Promotion
B4
Dispatch an Announcement
  1. Type: Announcement
  2. Details: Subject (e.g. "We are open late Fridays!") + Message Body. Leave expiration blank
  3. Delivery: Email + Push   Audience: All Members → 🚀 Send Promotion
Phase C — Verification & History
C1
Verify Campaign History
  1. Under Create a Promotion, switch to Campaign History sub-tab
  2. Verify all 5 entries appear — the 3 promotions + 1 announcement you created, plus the Initial Sign-Up Perk from merchant registration
  3. Check Type, Audience, and Expiry dates match what you entered
C2
Verify Member List Status
  1. Switch to Member List tab at the top of the dashboard
  2. Filter by "Status" — you should see entries with status "Created" or "Announcement"
