Part 2 · Member (Consumer) Sign-Up · Beta Test Guide

Consumer App Tester Guide

Requires completing Part 1 (Merchant Sign-Up) first — you need the QR code from that guide

👋 Welcome!

  • ⚠️ Complete Part 1 before starting this guide. You need the QR code from Part 1.
  • Use your iPhone with TestFlight installed
  • Estimated time: 5–8 minutes
  • Report bugs to hello@perkfinity.net
⚠️ Important: Do NOT tap "Activate This Offer" until you are ready to test the full redemption — the 5-minute timer starts immediately and cannot be paused.
Phase A — Install the App
A1
Scan the QR Code (Browser Redirect)

Open your iPhone camera and scan the QR code from your Part 1 countertop sign. The browser opens a Perkfinity landing page with the pending offer banner.

A2
Install via TestFlight
  1. Tap the Download on TestFlight banner
  2. If TestFlight is not installed, tap Install TestFlight → install from App Store → return to browser → tap the banner again
  3. In TestFlight, tap Install → Open
💡 You may briefly see "This beta isn't accepting new testers" — this is a cosmetic TestFlight quirk. The app still installs.
A3
Open the App — Confirm Home Screen

Tap Open in TestFlight. The app opens to the general Home screen (no pending offer yet — you're not signed in).

Phase B — Account Creation & Onboarding
B1
Camera Access Permission

First screen: camera permission dialog. Tap Allow Camera Access. Then tap Allow on the iOS native dialog too.

B2
Scan the QR Code in App

The QR scanner opens. Point your camera at the same merchant QR code from Part 1. Hold steady — the scanner detects it automatically and takes you to sign-up.

B3
Choose Sign-Up Method

Tap Sign Up with Email (recommended for this test). Three options are available: Apple, Google, and Email.

B4
Create Account — Email & Password
  • Email — real working address (e.g. your+test@gmail.com)
  • Password — min 8 chars, 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 number (e.g. TestPass1)
📝 Write down your credentials — you'll need them to log back in for Part 4.

Tap Create Account → when all checkmarks are green.

B5
Complete Your Profile

Fill in: Full Name, Phone (auto-formatted), City, ZIP Code (use real values for location matching). Tap Continue →.

B6
Location & Notification Permissions

Two pre-checked boxes: Enable Location and Allow Notifications. Leave both checked for testing. Tap Continue →.

B7
App Tour Slides

Three onboarding slides (Privacy-First, Redeem Instantly, One App Many Stores). Tap Continue → on each, then Get Started → (or tap Skip top-right).

Phase C — Home Screen & Discovery
C1
Explore the Home Screen
  • Top bar: Perkfinity logo + Sign Out button (top-right)
  • Available Perks: Gold banner card with pending offer — tap to view offers
  • Participating Merchants: Horizontal scroll — your scanned merchant appears first
  • Bottom Nav: 🏠 Home · 📷 Scan · 🕐 History · 👤 Profile
C2
View Your Available Offers

Tap the gold Available Offers banner to open the Offers page. You'll see: merchant name, offer title, expiry date (white/yellow/red), and the Activate This Offer button.

⚠️ Do not tap Activate until you're ready to test the full redemption now.
Phase D — Activate & Redeem
D1
Activate the Offer

Tap Activate This Offer. The Redemption Screen shows:

  • QR code with merchant logo in center
  • 6-digit fallback code (e.g. 690-649)
  • 5-minute countdown timer
⚠️ Timer starts immediately and cannot be paused.
D2
Mark as Redeemed

Tap the green Cashier: Mark as Redeemed button. A "✅ Successfully Redeemed!" confirmation appears. Tap any bottom tab to continue.

⚠️ Must be tapped before the timer hits 0:00.
Phase E — Verify History & Profile
E1
Check History Tab

Tap History tab → confirm the redeemed offer appears with merchant name, offer title, redemption timestamp, and status Redeemed.

E2
Review Profile Tab

Tap Profile tab → verify Name, Phone, City, ZIP are correct. Fields are editable — update any value, tap Continue → Permissions → Finish Setup to save.

💡 Sign Out is on the Home screen (top-right), not the Profile tab.
