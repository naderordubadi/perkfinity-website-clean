Getting Started

Open your web browser and go to perkfinity.net

From the top navigation bar, click on Merchant Sign Up

This will take you to the Merchant Form page where you'll complete 5 steps to set up your test store.

1
Step 1: Store/Business Details

Fill in the following information about your test store:

  • Store/Business Name * — Enter any test name (e.g. "My Test Bakery")
  • Street Address * — Use any real or test address
  • Suite / Unit # — Optional
  • City * — Use a real city (needed for the search test later)
  • State * — Select from dropdown
  • ZIP Code * — Must be a real, valid 5-digit ZIP code (e.g. 92692). This is required for the merchant search test later.
  • Contact Name * — Your name
  • Phone Number * — Format: xxx-xxx-xxxx (auto-formatted as you type)
  • Email Address * — Use a real, working email! This will be your merchant login username
  • Website — Optional
💡 Initial Sign-Up Perk: At the bottom of this step, you'll choose your New Member Perk. This is the welcome offer that will appear on your countertop QR sign. The default is "15% off purchase over $15" — you can keep this or select another option from the dropdown.

Click Continue to Tier Selection → to proceed.

2
Step 2: Choose Your Plan

Select one of two subscription tiers:

  • Trial Mode (selected by default) — Free
    No payment required until you hit 10 active members. Perfect for testing.
  • Tier 1 — $29.99/month
    Unlimited digital coupons & weekly newsletter inclusions.
💡 For Testing: Select Trial Mode. You won't be charged anything. The Trial plan automatically converts to Tier 1 when you reach 10 active members.

Click Continue → to proceed.

3
Step 3: Create Your Account

Your email from Step 1 is automatically used as your username. You'll see it displayed at the top of this step.

Set your login password:

  • Password * — Minimum 8 characters
  • Confirm Password * — Must match
💡 Password Requirements: Must contain at least 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, and 1 number. For example: TestPass1
📝 Write Down Your Credentials! Save your email (username) and password somewhere safe. You will need them to log in to the Merchant Dashboard later in this test.

Click Create Account & Continue → to proceed. The system will create your merchant account and you'll see a confirmation.

4
Step 4: Payment Method

This step securely collects payment information via Stripe.

💡 For Testing: This is currently pointing to the Stripe Test Environment. No real cards will be charged.

Please enter the following fake testing details:
  • Card Number — 4242 4242 4242 4242
  • Expiration — Any future date (e.g. 12/30)
  • CVC — Any 3 digits (e.g. 123)
  • ZIP — Any 5 digits (e.g. 12345)

If you selected Trial Mode in Step 2, you'll see a green notice explaining that you won't be billed until reaching 10 active members. The system simply saves the card on file for future use.

Click Save Payment Details. After the simulation completes, you'll automatically advance to Step 5.

5
Step 5: Customize Your Store/Business

Upload your store or business logo (optional). This logo will appear on:

  • Your Merchant Dashboard
  • The consumer app when members view your store
  • Your printable countertop QR sign (center of QR code)

Click Choose Image File to select an image (PNG, JPEG, or WebP). You can also change it after selecting by clicking Change Logo.

⚠️ For Testing: Please upload any image as your store logo — even a photo from your camera roll. This is important because we need to verify the logo appears correctly on the dashboard, in the QR sign, and in the consumer app.

Click Finish & Generate Sign → to complete the sign-up.

✓
Sign-Up Complete — Your QR Sign is Ready!

🎉 Congratulations! You've successfully set up your test merchant account.

You should now see your custom countertop display with:

  • The headline "Join Our Perks & Rewards Program!"
  • Your selected perk (e.g. "15% off purchase over $15")
  • A QR code that links directly to your store's membership page
  • Your store name at the bottom

Click the 🖨️ Print Countertop Sign button to print this sign — in a real scenario, the merchant would place this by their register.

⚠️ Save This Page! Click 🖨️ Print Countertop Sign and Save as PDF (or print it). You will need the QR code on this page for the next part of the test — scanning it with your phone to test the Member (consumer) experience.
📱 Next Step: Use your phone's camera to scan the QR code on this sign. This will take you to Part 2 of testing: the Member (Consumer) Experience, where you'll download the app, sign up as a customer, and activate the perk.
📊
Merchant Dashboard

You can access your Merchant Dashboard at any time by going to perkfinity.net and clicking Merchant Login in the top navigation.

Log in using the email and password you set in Step 3.

From the Dashboard you can:

  • View your Member List (members who scanned your QR code)
  • Update your Profile (logo, address, password)
  • Reprint your QR Sign from the Profile tab
  • Send Promotional Emails to your members
