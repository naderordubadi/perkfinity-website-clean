Returning Member Tester Guide

Requires completing Parts 1, 2, and 3 first — run as a separate session

👋 Welcome back!

  • ⚠️ Complete Parts 1, 2, and 3 before starting this guide.
  • You need an existing consumer account (Part 2) and an active campaign (Part 3)
  • Estimated time: 3–5 minutes
  • Report issues to hello@perkfinity.net
⚠️ Prerequisites: Have the app installed via TestFlight. Sign out of the app before starting.
Phase A — Returning Member Flow
A1
Scan the Merchant QR Code & Log In

Tests whether the app preserves a pending offer when the user logs in after scanning:

  1. Sign out — Sign Out button is on the Home screen (top-right)
  2. Scan the same merchant QR code from Part 1 with your iPhone camera
  3. On the onboarding slides, tap Skip or progress to the auth screen
  4. Tap "Log In" and enter your email and password
  5. Tap Log In
💡 Expected: After logging in, the Home screen immediately shows the pending offer banner.
A2
Loading & Offer Detection

After tapping Log In, watch for:

  • Log In button changes to "Please wait…" (loading feedback)
  • Home screen loads with the pending offer in Available Perks
⚠️ Check: Pending offer from QR scan appears in Available Perks — confirming QR context was preserved through log-in.
A3
Offer Expiration & Status Sync
  1. Note how many offers are listed in Available Perks before proceeding
  2. Tap your pending offer to open the details view
  3. Tap Activate Offer
  4. The 5-minute countdown begins — let it run out entirely (don't tap Done)
  5. Once expired, check the app first:
    • Tap History tab → confirm offer shows as Expired
    • Return to Home screen → confirm available offer count went down by 1
  6. Open Merchant Dashboard on computer → navigate to Member List → confirm status shows Expired
A4
Cancellation & Status Reversal
  1. Get to a pending offer:
    • If logged in — go to Home screen; pending offers are already listed
    • If not logged in — scan the merchant QR code and log in
  2. Tap the offer → tap Activate Offer to start the timer
  3. After a couple of seconds, tap Done or Cancel
⚠️ Check: Offer should revert to Available Perks — ensuring accidental activations can be undone.
A5
Background Interruption
  1. Get to a pending offer:
    • If logged in — go to Home screen; pending offers are already listed
    • If not logged in — scan the merchant QR code and log in
  2. Tap the offer → tap Activate Offer to start the timer
  3. While the timer is counting down, tap the Profile tab or navigate away
  4. Return to the Home screen
💡 Expected: App safely catches the interruption and reverts the offer to "Pending" state.
