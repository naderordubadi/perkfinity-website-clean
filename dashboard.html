Invite Your Existing Customers

Send your existing customers a one-time email through your own email platform (Gmail, Mailchimp, etc.). They join your Perkfinity member list instantly — no tech skills required.

Step 1 — Your Invite Link

Include this link as a button in your email. Recipients on their phone tap it to download the app and join your store instantly.

Loading...
Step 2 — Download & Attach Your QR Code

Download the QR image and attach it to your email before sending. Customers on a computer will scan it with their phone camera.

Step 3 — Copy the Email Template

Paste this into your email platform. Replace the bracketed fields with your details and insert your QR image and link where indicated.

✉️ Tips for Best Deliverability
  • Send from your own business email — not a generic address
  • Always include plain text alongside any images (never image-only emails)
  • Add alt text to the QR image: "Scan to join [Business Name] on Perkfinity"
  • Keep the subject line personal — avoid words like "Free" or "Limited Time" in the subject
