🔒 Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Perkfinity, operated by Safe Box Financial Technologies and Education LLC, a California limited liability company ("Perkfinity," "we," "our," or "us"). Perkfinity operates the website www.perkfinity.net, merchant and partner portals, and the Perkfinity mobile application (iOS and Android). We are committed to building a privacy-first ecosystem that connects people with local and online businesses through digital perks and rewards.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services.

🛡️ Our Privacy-First Promise: We never sell or rent your Personal Identifiable Information (PII). Any analytics we share with Merchants or third parties are strictly aggregated and anonymized. Most importantly, Merchants never see an individual Member's PII (such as your name, email address, phone number, live location, or individual demographic profile) through our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect different information depending on how you interact with Perkfinity — whether as a Member (consumer), a Merchant (business), or an Enterprise / Sales Representative partner.

A. For Members (Consumers)

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, and encrypted password when you create an account. If you sign in via Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address (or relay email), and authentication identifier from those providers.
  • Profile Information: During onboarding or in your Profile settings, we collect:
    • Phone Number: Collected for account verification, security, and duplicate/fraud prevention. Your phone number is never shared with any Merchant and is never used for third-party telemarketing.
    • City and 5-Digit ZIP Code: Used to surface relevant local deals and Daily Digest offers in your area.
    • Gender Selection: Members select from Female, Male, Non-binary, or Prefer not to answer. Selecting Prefer not to answer is always available if you choose not to disclose gender. Gender selections are used exclusively to generate strictly aggregated, anonymized demographic statistics and are never displayed on individual redemptions or linked to any personal identifier visible to a Merchant.
    • Notification Preferences: Your opt-in preferences for push notifications and email communications.
  • Activity Data: Records of merchant member lists you join and perks you claim, activate, and redeem — including QR code scans at participating physical or mobile locations, promo code reveals for online and hybrid merchants, and interactions with storefront links (such as website, online ordering, review/social links, Promotional Carousel banners, or VIP Full-Page Takeovers). This data is used to deliver your rewards and compute anonymized merchant performance metrics, and is never shared with Merchants in a manner that identifies you personally.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version, and push notification token if you opt in to receive push notifications.
  • Location Data (Interactive Map View & Nearby Distance): With your permission, both our mobile application and our website (/localperks) may request access to your device's or browser's real-time geolocation (GPS) solely to center our interactive Map View / Split View on your area, display nearby participating businesses, and calculate approximate mileage distances (mi) to perks. Real-time GPS coordinates are processed ephemerally in memory — Perkfinity does not store your GPS coordinates on our servers, does not track your location in the background, and never shares your live location with other users or Merchants. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device or browser settings and continue discovering deals manually by city or ZIP code.

B. For Merchants & Prospective Business Profiles

  • Merchant Registration Information: When you register or claim a business account, the information collected depends on your business presence type:
    • Physical Store merchants: Street address, suite, city, state, ZIP code, and optional website and social/review links.
    • Online Brand merchants: Business website URL (no physical street address required).
    • Online + Physical (Hybrid) merchants: Business website URL and, where applicable, storefront street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
    • Mobile / Pop-Up merchants: General operating city and ZIP area (no private residential or fixed street address required; map pins use general city/ZIP area centroids so private addresses are never exposed).
  • Contact & Account Credentials: Authorized contact person's name, business phone number, public customer-facing contact details (optional), login email address, and securely hashed portal password.
  • Business Assets: Logos, storefront/cover photos, campaign details, and custom promotional banners uploaded to your account.
  • Subscription & Billing Data: Selected subscription plan (such as Starter, Growth, or Scale), optional promotional Boost add-ons, and payment details processed directly by our PCI-compliant payment processor, Stripe (Perkfinity never stores full credit card numbers on our servers). Depending on your enrollment path or promotional code, billing begins upon account/application activation or upon reaching any applicable promotional member threshold displayed in your Merchant Dashboard.
  • Publicly Sourced Demonstration Profiles ("Pre-Setup Profiles"): To introduce local and online businesses to our platform, Perkfinity and its authorized independent sales representatives may compile publicly available business information — such as public business name, public storefront address, public business phone number or email, website URL, public review/social links, business category, and publicly displayed branding — to prepare temporary, unclaimed demonstration profiles ("Pre-Setup Profiles"). Unclaimed Pre-Setup Profiles use internal placeholder credentials until claimed by the business. If a prospective business chooses not to partner with Perkfinity — or upon request at any time to hello@perkfinity.net or (949) 414-4345 — Perkfinity promptly hides or permanently deletes the Pre-Setup Profile at zero cost.

C. For Enterprise Inquiries & Independent Sales Representatives

  • Enterprise Inquiries: Corporate contact details, brand/location counts, and operational requirements submitted voluntarily through our Enterprise inquiry form.
  • Independent Sales Representatives (1099 Contractors): Name, contact details, assigned referral codes/territories, and onboarding status. Legal agreement e-signatures, tax forms (W-9 / 1099-NEC), and commission payouts are processed directly through dedicated third-party compliance partners (Dropbox Sign, TaxBandits, and Stripe Connect); Perkfinity does not store raw Social Security Numbers or Taxpayer Identification Numbers on our servers.

D. Automatically Collected Information

  • Standard web and API request logs (IP address, browser type, operating system, referring/visited pages, and timestamps) for security, fraud prevention, and service reliability.
  • One-way cryptographic hashes of IP addresses (ip_hash) recorded alongside outbound merchant link clicks solely for anti-bot filtering and aggregated traffic analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing Services: Facilitating QR code perk scans, online promo code reveals, interactive map and nearby deal discovery, member list enrollment, and merchant campaign and analytics dashboards.
  • Perkfinity-Managed Communications: Sending transactional emails (welcome confirmations, password resets, billing notices), Perkfinity Daily Digest deal roundups, Perkfinity platform/community announcements, and opt-in push notifications. All member emails and push notifications are sent exclusively by Perkfinity — never directly by individual Merchants.
  • Account & Billing Administration: Managing Member profiles, Merchant subscriptions and optional promotional Boosts, Pre-Setup profile claims or removals, and independent representative attributions.
  • Aggregated Analytics & Platform Improvement: Generating strictly de-identified, aggregated usage, traffic, and demographic insights to help Merchants evaluate campaign ROI and help us improve platform performance.
  • Security & Legal Compliance: Detecting and preventing fraud, bot traffic, promo code abuse, and unauthorized access, and complying with applicable tax and legal obligations.

4. How We Protect and Share Your Information

The Perkfinity Privacy Promise

We do not sell, rent, or trade your Personal Identifiable Information (PII) to third parties, nor do we "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Any market or campaign insights shared externally are strictly in an aggregated, anonymized format that cannot reasonably be used to identify any individual.

What Merchants See

When a Member joins a Merchant's list, interacts with a storefront, or redeems a perk — whether by scanning a QR code in person or revealing a promo code online — the Merchant does not receive the Member's name, email address, phone number, live GPS coordinates, or individual gender selection. Merchants see only:

  • Aggregated campaign activity (e.g., "15 perks redeemed today" or "42 promo codes revealed this week")
  • Anonymous offer status counts (claimed, pending, redeemed, expired) with no personal identifiers
  • Total member counts and strictly aggregated, anonymized demographic breakdowns (such as aggregated Member gender totals and general ZIP/area distributions)
  • Aggregated outbound traffic counts (total clicks on website, online ordering, map directions, phone, review/social links, Promotional Carousel banners, and VIP Full-Page Takeovers)

Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted infrastructure and compliance providers strictly to operate Perkfinity:

  • OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap Contributors): Renders interactive map tiles on our website and mobile app. When you open Map View or Split View, your browser or device requests map vector tiles directly from OpenFreeMap using standard internet protocols (device IP address) with zero Perkfinity account or profile data attached.
  • Geoapify & U.S. Census Geocoder: Used strictly server-side by Perkfinity to convert Merchant business addresses (or general city/ZIP centroids for Mobile / Pop-Up businesses) into map pin coordinates when a store location is created or updated. Zero consumer GPS coordinates or Member personal data are ever sent to Geoapify or the U.S. Census Geocoder.
  • Brevo: Delivers Perkfinity's transactional emails, Daily Digest emails, and Perkfinity platform announcements. Your email address is processed by Brevo solely to deliver Perkfinity communications.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): Delivers opt-in push notifications to mobile devices using device push tokens.
  • Vercel & Neon: Cloud application hosting (Vercel) and encrypted PostgreSQL database hosting (Neon).
  • Apple & Google: Handles optional Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In authentication. Additionally, tapping directions inside a storefront launches native Apple Maps or Google Maps externally on your device without transmitting your Perkfinity account data.
  • Stripe: PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processor for Merchant subscriptions, promotional Boosts, and contractor Stripe Connect payouts.
  • Dropbox Sign & TaxBandits (Contractors Only): Used solely for independent sales representative contract e-signatures and IRS W-9 / 1099 tax compliance.

Security Measures

  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt (12 salt rounds)
  • All data in transit encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • JWT-based session authentication with token expiration and role-based access controls
  • Database encrypted at rest and one-way hashing (ip_hash) for click deduplication

5. Daily Digest & Platform Emails

Perkfinity sends a Daily Digest — an aggregated email highlighting active perks from participating Merchants — as well as occasional Perkfinity platform announcements. These emails:

  • Are sent directly by Perkfinity (from noreply@perkfinity.net or support@perkfinity.net) — never by or on behalf of any individual Merchant
  • Curate active offers relevant to Members based on general area (ZIP/city) and platform activity
  • Include a one-click unsubscribe link in compliance with the CAN-SPAM Act so you can opt out of promotional and digest emails at any time

Your email address is never disclosed to any Merchant, and Merchants cannot initiate direct individual emails to Members.

6. Promo Codes

For Online Brand and Hybrid merchants, Perkfinity generates promo codes linked to the Merchant's offer. When a Member reveals a promo code on our platform:

  • The Member receives the code to apply at the Merchant's online checkout or point of sale.
  • Perkfinity logs the anonymous claim event for campaign reporting without disclosing the Member's identity to the Merchant.

7. Push Notifications

If you opt in on our mobile app, we may send push notifications regarding new perks from Merchants you follow, expiring offers, pop-up location updates from Mobile Merchants, and important account updates. You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's Settings.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Update or Correct Profile Data: Members and Merchants can review and update their profile information (including changing a Member Gender selection to Prefer not to answer) at any time in their account settings.
  • Opt Out of Marketing Emails: Click the one-click "unsubscribe" link in any promotional or Daily Digest email, or email us at hello@perkfinity.net.
  • Disable Notifications or Location Access: Turn off push notifications or revoke GPS/browser location permissions at any time in your device or browser settings.
  • Prospective Business Pre-Setup Removal / Hiding: Any business owner whose publicly available business details appear in an unclaimed Pre-Setup demonstration profile may request immediate hiding or permanent removal at any time by contacting hello@perkfinity.net or (949) 414-4345.
  • Data Access & Portability: Request a copy or export of the personal information we maintain about you.
  • Instant Account & Data Deletion: Members can permanently delete their account and wipe their PII instantly inside the Perkfinity mobile app (Profile → Delete Account). Merchants have a dedicated account deletion workflow inside the Billing tab of their Merchant Dashboard. All deletion workflows comply with applicable federal and state privacy laws (including CCPA/CPRA) and Apple App Store / Google Play requirements.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our Services and meet legal, tax, or accounting obligations. Upon account deletion or cancellation:

  • Members: Account Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is permanently wiped immediately upon executing account deletion.
  • Merchants: To prevent accidental loss of business configurations and campaign history during seasonal pauses, cancelled Merchant accounts enter an automatic 6-month Grace Period following subscription expiration. Merchants may bypass this grace period and permanently wipe their account PII immediately using the manual deletion tool in the Billing tab of their dashboard. Unclaimed Pre-Setup demonstration profiles that do not proceed to partnership are hidden or deleted by Perkfinity.
  • Aggregated Analytics: Strictly anonymized, de-identified statistical records (containing zero personal identifiers) are retained for historical platform and merchant analytics.

10. Children's Privacy & Age Assurance

Perkfinity's Services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 where required by applicable law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Where supported by your mobile operating system or required by applicable law (including the California Digital Age Assurance Act, AB 1043), the Perkfinity mobile app may check a privacy-preserving age-range signal provided by your device's operating system (Apple or Google) in real time solely to verify minimum age eligibility — without collecting or storing your date of birth or government identification. If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of service providers with whom we disclose it.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • No Sale or Cross-Context Behavioral Sharing: Perkfinity does not "sell" or "share" (for cross-context behavioral advertising) your personal information.
  • Sensitive Personal Information Limitation: Account login credentials and optional demographic selections (such as gender) are used strictly to provide requested Services, maintain security, and compute de-identified aggregate statistics as permitted under Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.121(d), and are never used to infer personal characteristics or build behavioral advertising profiles.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.
